FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What does a freelance enterprise architect do?
A freelance enterprise architect designs cloud and technology strategies, conducts architecture reviews, evaluates vendors, and guides digital transformation — without the overhead of a full-time hire or a large consulting firm. Engagements are scoped to a project, retainer, or interim period, which means you get senior expertise exactly when you need it and nothing more.
Can a small business in India afford cloud consulting?
Yes. Unlike enterprise consulting firms, an independent architect scopes work to your actual budget and problem. No bloated retainers, no solutions built for a 5,000-person enterprise being sold to a 20-person team. Every engagement starts with a free 30-minute discovery call — no commitment.
What is on-job support for IT professionals?
On-job support is 1-on-1 mentoring built around your live work — not generic courses. A senior architect reviews your actual designs, helps you navigate real project decisions, and gives you the architecture perspective your manager assumes you already have.
How long does a career shift to cloud architecture take in India?
With a structured program covering cloud fundamentals, solution design, and hands-on AWS/Azure labs, most motivated learners are interview-ready within 6 to 12 months. Developers and those with some IT background move faster; professionals shifting from non-tech roles like banking or operations typically need the full 12 months.
Do you offer GenAI and agentic AI consulting?
Yes. GenAI adoption is one of the fastest-growing areas of enterprise demand right now. Services include RAG pipeline design, LLM integration architecture, AI platform strategy, and agentic AI readiness assessments — all scoped for production deployment, not just prototypes.
