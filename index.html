For Small Businesses · India

Technology that actually fits your business

Most IT vendors sell small businesses solutions built for corporations. I start with your problem — inventory, billing, customer reach, or operations — and design something proportionate and affordable.

  • Current-state audit — what you have and what's slowing you down
  • Lean cloud or software solution design (no over-engineering)
  • Vendor selection guidance & implementation oversight
  • Ongoing advisory — you keep the relationship, not a ticket system

Good fit: Retail, logistics, services, or manufacturing businesses in India with 5–200 employees looking to modernise without burning budget.

For Working Professionals

On-the-job support when the work gets real

Being thrown into a new role — or a new team — with no senior guidance is more common than it should be. I give you the architecture perspective your manager assumes you already have.

  • 1-on-1 sessions around your live work problems
  • Code & design reviews with actionable feedback
  • Architecture decision guidance (ADRs, trade-off analysis)
  • Interview and promotion prep — how to communicate design clearly

Good fit: Developers, tech leads, or recently promoted architects at 1–5 years experience who want a senior sounding board without the politics of asking a colleague.

For Students & Career Shifters ← Primary

From where you are to where tech is hiring

The gap between college or your current role and a cloud/architecture career is smaller than it looks — if you learn the right things in the right order. I've seen what enterprises actually hire for, and I'll close that gap with you.

  • Structured curriculum: Cloud fundamentals → Solution design → Real projects
  • Hands-on labs on AWS / Azure — no death by PowerPoint
  • Resume, LinkedIn, and portfolio review aligned to current hiring patterns
  • Mock interviews & system design walkthroughs
  • Small cohorts (max 8) so questions actually get answered

Good fit: Final-year students, recent graduates, or professionals in non-tech roles (banking, ops, QA) targeting a cloud or architecture career within 6–12 months.
